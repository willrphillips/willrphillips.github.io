GOB Books: Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-09-05.

GOB Books reads accounting data (transactions, accounts, balances, reports) from QuickBooks Online for companies its operator has authorized. It reads nothing else and writes nothing back.

Data is stored only on a server the operator controls, is encrypted in transit, and is used only to produce internal financial summaries for the owners of those companies. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

OAuth tokens are stored server-side and can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the app in the Intuit account settings, after which no further data is read. Stored data can be deleted on request to the operator.
